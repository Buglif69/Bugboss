# Leaflet Drop Brief — For Ashleigh

**Project:** Spring 2026 letterbox campaign — 80,000 pamphlets, two waves
**Goal:** 150+ enquiries, 100+ booked jobs, every enquiry tracked by suburb
**Full strategy:** see `BRISBANE-LEAFLET-DROP-STRATEGY.md` in this repo
**Deadline pressure:** Wave 1 must land letterboxes in the first two weeks of September — printing and distributor booking need to be locked in by ~22 August.

---

## Your job in one line

Book the distributor, get the flyer artwork updated and printed in suburb-coded versions, set up the tracking, and have Wave 1 (40,000 flyers) dropping in Tier 1 suburbs by the first week of September.

---

## Task 1 — Get 3 distributor quotes (this week)

Request quotes from at least three Brisbane letterbox distribution companies (e.g. Australia Print Group, Direct Mail Solutions, Ivy/local walker networks — Google "letterbox distribution Brisbane" and pick three with real reviews).

**Every quote must confirm, in writing:**
- [ ] GPS tracking of walkers + photo proof of delivery
- [ ] Price per 1,000 for **shared** run AND **solo/priority** run (we want solo in our top suburbs so we're not buried in a bundle of 20 flyers)
- [ ] They can deliver to **detached houses only** — no unit blocks, no townhouse letterbox banks, skip "no junk mail" boxes
- [ ] Per-suburb letterbox counts for the suburb list below (they all have this data)
- [ ] They can split the job into two waves (Sept and late Oct/Nov) and deliver **Thursday/Friday** so flyers land before the weekend
- [ ] Can they handle different flyer versions per suburb zone? (needed for tracking — see Task 3)

**Budget guardrail:** shared runs should be ~$109–$150 per 1,000; solo ~$189 per 1,000. Total distribution budget target: **$10k–$14k for all 80k**. Anything above that, flag with Jason before booking.

---

## Task 2 — Flyer artwork changes (before print!)

Do NOT print the current flyer as-is. The "starting from $220" lead is the weakest thing on it — competitors letterbox "from $129". Get these changes made:

1. **New headline offer (Wave 1 version):**
   > **Spring Special: FREE termite check with every general pest treatment** — book by 31 October
2. One line justifying our price: *"Family & pet-safe treatment — internal, external + roof void. No cheap spray-and-dash."*
3. **Trust strip:** ⭐ 1,300+ five-star reviews · Family owned · 20 years in Brisbane · 30-day guarantee · 12-month termite warranty
4. **Big phone number** (07) 3113 9666 + **QR code** to a booking page
5. The offer deadline (31 October) must be clearly visible
6. Wave 2 gets a different headline (design it in October): *"Flying termites are swarming Brisbane — get a free termite check before they cost you $30,000"*

Confirm final artwork with Jason before it goes to print.

---

## Task 3 — Tracking setup (non-negotiable)

We're finding our best-converting suburbs with this drop, so every enquiry must be traceable:

- [ ] Ask the printer to zone-version the QR code — one QR per suburb cluster (5 clusters, listed below). It's a cheap print variation. Each QR points to the booking page with a tracking tag, e.g. `slugabug.com.au/spring?s=forestlake`
- [ ] Add offer code **"SPRING"** to the flyer so phone callers can be attributed too
- [ ] Set up in ServiceM8: every new enquiry gets source = **"Leaflet"** + the suburb recorded. Brief whoever answers the phones: always ask *"Which suburb are you in, and how did you hear about us?"*
- [ ] Build a simple spreadsheet: suburb | flyers dropped | enquiries | booked jobs | revenue. Update weekly.

## Task 4 — The drop plan

### Wave 1 — first two weeks of September (~40,000 flyers, Tier 1 only)

| Cluster (QR zone) | Suburbs |
|---|---|
| A — Forest Lake | Forest Lake, Heathwood, Doolandella |
| B — Carindale belt | Carindale, Belmont, Mansfield, Wishart |
| C — Calamvale | Calamvale, Sunnybank Hills, Stretton, Parkinson |
| D — Logan corridor | Springwood, Shailer Park, Daisy Hill, Rochedale South |
| E — Timber belt | Holland Park, Camp Hill, Carina, Annerley, Moorooka, Salisbury |

Split the 40k across clusters based on the distributor's letterbox counts. Pay for **solo runs** in clusters A–D if the budget allows.

### Wave 2 — late October / early November (~40,000 flyers)

- Re-drop the top-performing Wave 1 clusters (check the tracking spreadsheet mid-October with Jason)
- Add from Tier 2 as needed: Alexandra Hills, Capalaba, Kenmore, Chapel Hill, Fig Tree Pocket, Aspley, Bridgeman Downs, Albany Creek, Murrumba Downs, Kallangur, Petrie
- Use the Wave 2 termite-swarm artwork

### Never drop in
West End / South Brisbane / Woolloongabba unit areas, Fortitude Valley, Newstead, St Lucia student flats, or estates newer than ~5 years.
