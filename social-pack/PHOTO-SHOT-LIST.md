# Slug-A-Bug — Photo Shot List (matched to every post)

Take the photo → name it exactly the **filename** shown (e.g. `post-012.jpg`) → send it to Claude
or drop it in `social-pack/src/photos/` → that post automatically re-renders with your real photo
as the background, under a brand colour wash that keeps the text readable.

## How to shoot (applies to every photo)

- **Landscape, phone camera is fine** — highest quality setting.
- **Put the subject centre or slightly RIGHT of frame** — the headline text sits on the left/top.
- **Uniform + PPE on** in every shot with a person — it's a trust signal.
- Bright natural light for "light" posts; torch-lit / moody is perfect for night & roof-void shots.
- **Never include:** customer faces, kids' faces, house numbers, or anything identifying a client's address.
- One photo can be reused on several posts — just copy it to multiple filenames (see the Core 20 below).
