# BUG BOSS Strategy: 80,000-Pamphlet Brisbane Leaflet Drop (Spring 2026)

## Bottom Line

Yes — coming into spring is the single best window of the year to drop pest-control leaflets in Brisbane. Termite swarming season runs late spring into summer (roughly November–January, triggered by heat, humidity and storms), and general pest activity (cockroaches, ants, spiders) wakes up from September. The play is NOT one giant 80k dump: split into **two waves of ~40k hitting the same 10–12 high-scoring suburbs twice** — Wave 1 late August/early September, Wave 2 late October/early November right before storm-and-swarm season. Repetition beats reach in letterbox marketing.

Use GPS-tracked walker distribution (~$109–$189 per 1,000), not Australia Post unaddressed mail (~29–34c per box = $23k+ for 80k). Total distribution spend lands around **$9,000–$15,000** for all 80,000.

---

## Why Spring Is the Right Call (Timing Research)

- **Termite swarming (alates/flying termites)** in Brisbane occurs late spring to early summer, especially after storms and humid evenings — this is when homeowners SEE termites and panic-call. Sources: Termite Guys Brisbane, Conquer Termites.
- **General pests** (cockroaches, ants, spiders, mozzies) ramp from September as temperatures rise — the classic "book a spray before summer" season.
- Industry guidance: schedule annual termite inspections in **late spring, just before swarming season** — so a flyer landing Sept–Nov catches people at exactly the right moment.
- A flyer that lands in August/September sits on the fridge when the first warm humid night sends cockroaches across the kitchen floor. That's the conversion mechanism — leaflets for services are a "keep until trigger event" medium, not an impulse buy.

**Verdict: drop window = last week of August through mid-November. You are starting at the perfect time.**

## Who Actually Reads Letterbox Mail (Targeting the Right Households)

Research findings:

- ~**81% of Australian households do NOT have a "no junk mail" sticker** (~19% do) — Clean Up Australia / Sustainability Victoria.
- Catalogue/letterbox readership is huge — Roy Morgan found ~70% catalogue readership; more Australians read the printed Aldi catalogue than a printed newspaper. 83% of people carry unaddressed material into the house.
- Readership skews to: **owner-occupiers, families with kids, and older residents in detached houses**. These are also the exact people who PAY for pest control.
- The people who bin junk mail unread skew to: **renters, apartment dwellers, inner-city young professionals**. Renters also don't buy pest control — that's the landlord's problem.

### Targeting rules (give these to the distributor)

1. **Detached houses only** — skip apartment blocks, unit complexes and townhouse letterbox banks entirely.
2. **High owner-occupier suburbs** (65%+ owner-occupied).
3. **Established family suburbs** with older housing stock (post-war timber, Queenslanders, 1970s–90s brick-veneer with timber roof frames) — highest termite anxiety and highest willingness to pay.
4. **Bushland / creek / golf-course adjacency** — termite pressure corridors.
5. Skip: CBD, inner-city unit precincts, high-renter student areas, new estates under 5 years old (still under builder termite warranty, fewer pests).

## Budget & ROI Model

### Costs (80,000 flyers)

| Item | Rate | Total |
|---|---|---|
| Distribution — shared walker run, GPS-tracked | ~$109–$150 /1,000 | $8,700–$12,000 |
| Distribution — solo run upgrade for Tier 1 (recommended: flyer isn't buried in a bundle of 20) | ~$189 /1,000 | +$2,000–$3,000 on Tier 1 only |
| Australia Post unaddressed (comparison — not recommended at this volume) | 29–34c /box | $23,200–$27,200 |

**Recommended spend: ~$10,000–$14,000** — shared runs in Tier 2, solo/priority runs in Tier 1, always GPS-tracked with photo proof.

### Returns (be conservative — plan on these, celebrate above them)

Unaddressed service flyers realistically pull **0.1%–0.3%** enquiry rates (the "1–3%" some distributors quote is retail-offer territory; don't budget on it).

| Response rate | Enquiries from 80k | Booked jobs (~65% close) | Round-1 revenue @ ~$270 avg general job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.1% (floor) | 80 | ~52 | ~$14,000 |
| 0.2% (likely) | 160 | ~104 | ~$28,000 |
| 0.3% (good creative + good targeting) | 240 | ~156 | ~$42,000 |

**The real ROI is behind the first job:** every general-pest customer is an annual repeat (~$250+/yr), a termite-inspection upsell (~$250–$330), and a small % convert to termite treatments/barriers ($2,000–$5,000+). At 100 new customers, 12-month value is realistically **$45k–$70k+** against ~$12k distribution + print. Even the floor scenario roughly breaks even in round one and profits on LTV.

## The Flyer Itself — Fix the "From $220" Problem

Competitors letterbox and advertise "from $129–$150" (e.g. Tom's from $129). Leading your flyer with "from $220" makes you the expensive option in a 3-second glance. **Don't drop your real price — change the hook:**

1. **Lead with the offer, not the base price.** Options (pick one):
   - "**Spring Special: FREE termite check with every general pest treatment** — booked before Oct 31" (costs you 20 min on site, feeds the high-value termite pipeline)
   - "Pest + termite inspection bundle — save $80 this spring"
   - "$0 call-out + 30-day re-treat guarantee"
2. **Justify the price in one line:** "Family & pet-safe treatment, internal + external + roof void — no cheap spray-and-dash."
3. **Trust strip:** ⭐ 1,300+ five-star reviews · family owned · 20 years in Brisbane · 30-day guarantee · 12-month termite warranty.
4. **Deadline:** every leaflet offer needs an expiry — "book by 31 October" — or it sits on the fridge forever.
5. **One giant phone number + QR code** to a dedicated landing page (slugabug.com.au/spring) — big enough to read from the bin. 😄
6. **Fridge-worthiness:** consider a "Brisbane Spring Pest Calendar" panel (what swarms when) — useful content survives the junk-mail cull.

## Attribution — Know Which Suburbs Convert (Non-Negotiable)

You can't find "the most converting suburbs" without measuring. On every flyer batch:

- **Unique QR code / UTM per suburb cluster** (e.g. slugabug.com.au/spring?s=forestlake) — printers can zone-version cheaply.
- **Offer code** ("mention SPRING for the free termite check") so phone bookings attribute too.
- **ServiceM8: log every enquiry's suburb + source = "Leaflet"** — kill the "Other/Unknown" attribution leak.
- After Wave 1, rank suburbs by enquiries per 1,000 dropped → Wave 2 doubles down on winners and cuts losers.
